# Spec 0008

Story: none (ribo:tweak fast-path; Issue #94, filed by the dep-scanner scout)

## Path selection

`dep-bump`. This is a dependency-version tweak, not a feature. Scope is
deliberately the low-risk PATCH + MINOR group only. The MAJOR group from the
Issue is intentionally deferred (see "Scope decision" below).

## Scope decision

The Issue proposes three groups of bumps. The researcher confirmed package.json
uses caret ranges whose floors sit below the scanner's reported "current"
versions, so:

- The PATCH + MINOR group already falls within the existing caret ranges. It is
  satisfiable by pinning the new minimums in package.json and regenerating the
  lockfile. Typecheck + verify safe; no source or test edits expected.
- The MAJOR group (@types/node 26, @vitejs/plugin-react 6, happy-dom 20,
  typescript 7, vite 8, vitest 4, zod 4) all crosses a caret boundary and
  carries real breaking-change risk across the typecheck, test, verify, and
  build pipeline (typescript 5->7 and vitest 2->4 are the highest-risk; zod 3->4
  would touch the `ZodTypeAny` import in `src/verify/core/types.ts`). Those are
  NOT tweak-sized and are held out of this change. They should be handled
  separately, one ecosystem/major at a time, with their own review.

This tweak ships only the safe group, matching the scanner's own guidance
("merge the patch group first, then minor, then major if any").

## Risks

- Regression risk from the bump: mitigated by running typecheck + test + verify
  after the change; all three must pass or the builder reports and does not
  proceed.
- Registry reachability: the lockfile regen needs network access to the npm
  registry. If unreachable in CI, the builder must report it plainly rather than
  hand-edit the lockfile.
- Oversizing avoided by scoping out the MAJOR group.
