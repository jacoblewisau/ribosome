2. Fewer "is this okay?" pop-ups

The bot stops asking permission for boring plans, and still stops for the real ones.

ribosome-bot botcommented just now
Plan is ready and needed no decisions from you, so I am building it now. Want to change anything? Reply /changes and I will pull it back.

Why better: nothing for you to do. You do not read a brief, you do not type a password. You can close the tab.

ribosome-bot botcommented just now
I need your OK on one thing before building:

This feature sends email from a new address (notifications@yourapp.com).

or reply /approve

Why still safe: email, money, and private data always stop the bot. It boils the question down to the one thing that needs a human.

ribosome-bot botcommented just now

Spec for review (gate 2)

Data model: adds a theme field to user settings.
Files: src/settings/Theme.tsx, src/settings/store.ts, src/settings/Theme.verify.ts
Data flow: toggle writes to local store, persists to the settings API on change.
Tests: ThemeToggle.spec.ts asserts the choice survives a reload. Risks: none flagged.
Reply /approve to advance, or /changes <note> to revise.

The cost: this same wall of text appears for every plan, even when there is nothing to decide. So you skim it and type the password out of habit.
